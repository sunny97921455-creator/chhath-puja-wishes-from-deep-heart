What is Chhath Puja?

छठ महापर्व का महत्व

Chhath Puja is an ancient Hindu festival dedicated to Surya Dev (the Sun God) and Chhathi Maiya. It is celebrated with immense faith, devotion and purity.

Devotees express gratitude to the Sun for sustaining life and pray for the happiness and well-being of their families.

02 / 07
☀️

Worship of Surya Dev

सूर्य देव की उपासना

The Sun is the source of light, energy and life. Chhath Puja reminds us to respect nature and be thankful for the blessings it gives us.

Devotees offer Arghya to the setting and rising sun with deep devotion and gratitude.

03 / 07
🙏

Chhathi Maiya

छठी मैया का आशीर्वाद

Chhathi Maiya is deeply revered during this festival. Devotees pray for the health, happiness, protection and prosperity of their children and families.

The festival represents faith, love, dedication and the power of devotion.

04 / 07
🍃

Nahay Khay

नहाय-खाय

Nahay Khay marks the beginning of the four-day Chhath festival.

Devotees take a holy bath, clean their surroundings and prepare a simple, pure meal. It represents cleanliness, discipline and preparation for the sacred celebration.

05 / 07
🪔

Kharna

खरना

Kharna is the second day of Chhath Puja. Devotees observe a day-long fast and worship with sincerity.

In the evening, traditional offerings such as kheer and roti are prepared and shared as Prasad. It symbolizes devotion, gratitude and sharing.

06 / 07
🌇

Sandhya Arghya

संध्या अर्घ्य

On the third day, devotees gather at rivers, ponds and other water bodies to offer Arghya to the setting sun.

The beautiful sight of diyas, traditional baskets and prayers creates an atmosphere filled with faith and devotion.

07 / 07
🌄

Usha Arghya

उषा अर्घ्य

On the final morning, devotees offer Arghya to the rising sun.

It symbolizes new beginnings, hope, positivity and gratitude. After the offering, the fast is traditionally concluded.
